Just-in-time indexing lets AI agents answer ad hoc onchain questions by building datasets on demand instead of maintaining permanent infrastructure. The approach uses Envio's free tier to backfill data in minutes and then delete the indexer after use, inverting the traditional workflow where questions follow data availability. A Curve Finance researcher calls it "one of the simplest ways to get large datasets of onchain data for our research purposes."
