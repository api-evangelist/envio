Solana support in HyperIndex is now stable. Five HyperIndex releases from v3.10.0 to v3.13.0 add automatic multiprocess indexing, a Rust RPC source and per-chain rollbacks. New tutorials cover Arc, Robinhood Chain, HyperEVM and Solana, alongside docs for Envio Cloud troubleshooting and trace decoding, and a page comparing Envio with other indexers.
